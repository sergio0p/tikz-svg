Watercolor — Fill vs Stroke

Each page shows the same motif painted two ways: as a filled wash (the region flooded with pigment) and as a stroked wash (only the outline / spine swept into a ribbon of pigment).

Click the side that looks better on each page — your picks are tallied at the end to settle which treatment wins as the default. Sides are randomized per page, and the paint is freshly generated on every reload.
