Watercolor washes

Algorithmic wash — each outline is deformed (recursive midpoint jitter) into 10 translucent copies stacked so overlaps build up and darken; no stamps, pure SVG. Fills wash the shape interior; strokes sweep the path into a tapered ribbon and wash that. Each seed is a fixed painting that stays put; Re-roll shows the family of paintings the same parameters produce. Left = crisp, right = washed (same geometry).
