Watercolor — Wet-blend Audition

Each page shows the same pair of overlapping washes, rendered two ways. One side is wet union (the two pools merge — the overlap stays one tone). The other is multiply glaze (the washes stay separate — the overlap darkens where they cross).

Click the side that looks more like real watercolor on each page — your picks are tallied at the end to settle which approach wins. Sides are randomized per page, and the paint pattern is freshly generated on every reload.
