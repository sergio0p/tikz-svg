Watercolor brush strokes

Every mark here is a stroke — a centerline path swept into a tapered ribbon, then dissolved into translucent layers that stack and darken at overlaps. No fills, no fonts: the word below is six painted spines fed to render() as config.paths with decoration: { type:'watercolor' }. Each seed is one fixed painting; Re-roll shows the family of paintings the same brush produces.
